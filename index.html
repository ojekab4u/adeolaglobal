What We Offer

Our Products

We supply premium hardwood charcoal in various grades and a range of agricultural commodities for local and international markets.

Shisha Charcoal

Premium charcoal designed for shisha/hookah use. Clean burn, minimal ash, and long-lasting heat.

BBQ Charcoal

High-heat, long-burning hardwood charcoal perfect for grilling and outdoor cooking.

Non-Sparkling Charcoal

Safe, spark-free charcoal ideal for indoor use, restaurants, and food service applications.

Finger Charcoal

Convenient finger-sized charcoal pieces, easy to light and perfect for everyday use.

Agricultural Commodities

We export a variety of agricultural products including cashew nuts, cocoa, sesame seeds, and other cash crops — sourced from trusted local farmers and prepared to international standards.
